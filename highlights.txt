Time-Aware Dual-Stream Masked Autoencoder for Protocol-Agnostic Intrusion
Detection in Automotive Ethernet

Yeonjae Kang, Huy Kang Kim, Seonghoon Jeong

- Transformer and CNN streams jointly model packet bytes and inter-packet deltas.
- Training uses only normal traffic, with no attack labels and no protocol parsing.
- Time-aware positional encoding from packet timing widens the stable threshold range.
- F1 reaches 0.9808, and AUROC 0.9974 essentially matches a fully supervised baseline.
- This is the first anomaly detection study on the real-vehicle CarDS dataset.
